How can we assist?

Get answers to frequently asked questions about your journey and dining experience.

Ordering

Yes! You can pre-order your meals up to 48 hours before your departure. Simply enter your train number and seat details during checkout to ensure synchronization with your transit schedule.

Dietary filters are available on the restaurant listings. Once you select an item, you can add specific instructions for the chef regarding allergies or preferences in the 'Special Requests' field.

Payments

We accept all major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and TransitFood vouchers. We do not accept cash on delivery due to security protocols in transit hubs.

Deliveries

Our system tracks train schedules in real-time. If your train is delayed by more than 15 minutes, we notify the restaurant to adjust preparation times. Your delivery will be synchronized with the new arrival time.

Delivery points vary by station. Usually, it is either your designated coach entrance or a specific TransitFood kiosk on the platform. Check your tracking link for the precise location map.
